# Griabuntu Mail artwork

Confirmed by Markus Kainer on 2026-10-27: the six PNG originals in
`design/owner-artwork-20261027/` were created with ChatGPT from text prompts
only, without uploaded third-party pictures, logos or font references.
The selected app/tray icon is the closed coloured envelope. The banner is
`brand-banner.png`. Original bytes and SHA-256 hashes remain preserved.

The owner authorised these assets for this fork, its GitHub presentation and
its Debian packages. The current originals and their technical exports are
distributed with this GPL-3.0-only application under the same licence to the
extent copyright or related rights subsist. This is not a claim of exclusive
human authorship or of trademark clearance. Historical CC0 studies retain
their existing terms; they are not the source of the current icon.

The app SVG embeds the supplied PNG; it is not an editable vector recreation.
Raster icon sizes and the monochrome alpha-mask export are technical
derivatives, not replacement drawings. The banner is included byte-for-byte
in the app resources. See `brand/README.md` and `build-aux/render-brand.py`.
